These essays represent things that I am curious about, that I have researched to some extent and formalized into articles and essays are a personal summary of what i want to remember or share with someone to start off further discussion. These essasy do not neccessarily form a coherent opinion and they may range from speculation to epistemic arguments. Whether they meet any consistent quality bar is for the reader to decide. All essays have been processed by a LLM to perform various changes, transformations and corrections. In most cases the LLM was used as a shortcut to create a readable synthesis of notes, for fact checking and exploration of counterfactuals. The substantive basis of all essays is my own and represent my judgement of what is important.
